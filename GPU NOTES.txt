GPU NOTES:
- For Sprite Addressing:
- - SpriteNumber | PixelNumber
- - PixelNumber consists of 6 bits. 3 bits for horizontal counter, 3 bits for vertical counter.
- - LSB is the LSB of the horizontal counter. hPos is followed by vPos.


RAM ICs:
- Sprite Sheet: AS6C62256-55PCN
- Buffer Memory: AS6C4008
